# Real Estate JV Tax Research - 2026-06-03

This folder collects primary-source materials for modeling a U.S. real estate flipping venture with two U.S. partners and two foreign partners.

## Contents

- source-files/ - downloaded PDFs from IRS and Treasury sources.
- extracted-text/ - searchable text extracted from the PDFs using pdftotext.
- structuring-memo.md - working analysis, risk model, and questions for counsel.

## Key Source Files

- IRS Publication 515: withholding on nonresident aliens and foreign entities.
- IRS Publication 519: U.S. tax guide for aliens.
- Form 1120 and instructions: U.S. C corporation return.
- Form 5472 and instructions: 25% foreign-owned U.S. corporation reporting.
- Forms 1042 and 1042-S: withholding reporting for U.S.-source payments to foreign persons.
- W-8BEN and W-8BEN-E packages: foreign beneficial owner and treaty documentation.
- Forms 8288, 8288-A, 8288-B and instructions: FIRPTA withholding and withholding certificate process.
- Form 8990 and instructions: Section 163(j) business interest limitation.
- Form 706-NA and instructions: estate tax return for nonresident noncitizens.
- U.S.-Belgium income tax treaty, protocol, and technical explanation.
- U.S.-U.K. income tax treaty, protocol, notes, and technical explanation.

## Important Limits

This research folder is not a legal opinion. The final structure should be reviewed by U.S., Belgian, U.K., and UAE counsel as applicable, especially for treaty residence, limitation-on-benefits, anti-conduit rules, debt/equity classification, transfer pricing, beneficial ownership, estate tax, and state tax filings.
